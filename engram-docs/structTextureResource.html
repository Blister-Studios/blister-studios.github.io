Detailed Description

Represents a texture asset loaded into GPU memory.

  • A TextureResource acts as a managed handle to a GPU-resident image.
  • It stores both the primary sg_image and a persistent sg_view,
  • allowing the renderer to bind and sample the texture instantly during
  • a draw call without needing to perform resource pool lookups.
